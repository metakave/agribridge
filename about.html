About AgriBridge

Connecting Verified Origins with Import Markets Worldwide

AgriBridge is an agricultural commodity trading and supply company connecting verified agricultural producers and established supply origins with import markets worldwide.

Cross-Border Sourcing

Essential Food Commodities Delivered with Integrity

We focus on the cross-border sourcing, import, and supply of essential food commodities—specifically grains and cereals, pulses and legumes, and oilseeds and meals.

Working directly with vetted producers, millers, processors, established exporters, and international trading houses across major agricultural origins—including North & South America, the Black Sea region, Australia, and South & Southeast Asia—we supply industrial buyers, food processors, and importers across South Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and Southeast Asia.

Execution Excellence

Operational Capabilities

Disciplined cross-border infrastructure engineered to fulfill physical commodity contracts with transparency and reliability.

Established Sourcing Network

Direct commercial sourcing from verified agricultural producers, regional millers, processors, and established exporters in key origin markets.

Bulk & Containerized Execution

Operational capability to handle both containerized shipments (FCL) and dry bulk vessel cargoes under FOB, CFR, and CIF terms, tailored to buyer volume and port infrastructure.

Core Commodity Specialization

Focused trading expertise in milling and feed wheat, parboiled and white rice, yellow corn, soybeans, soybean meal, and major pulse varieties.

Quality Assurance & Independent Inspection

Weight, grade, moisture, and cargo specifications are verified prior to shipment through recognized independent inspection agencies—such as SGS or Intertek—as required by contract terms and destination regulations.

Trade & Regulatory Compliance

Complete management of international shipping and customs documentation, ensuring smooth clearance at loading and discharge ports.
